Help

What does the membership include?

$9.99/month opens every city, the full records past the free preview, and downloads. Each new week is included while the membership is active. Cancel anytime from Billing.

How do alerts work?

A paid member saves one alert for a city, section, permit type, trade, or size. The local job checks twice a day and emails a short sample when something new matches. The sample links back to the filtered list.

How do I download records?

Open a city on the index and use Download. Members can take the filtered list as a spreadsheet. Guests see the sign-in offer.

I paid and cannot sign in.

Use the email and password chosen on the subscribe page. If that email already had an account, sign in with the existing password. For a payment that did not attach, write to sales@businesslocalrecords.com.
