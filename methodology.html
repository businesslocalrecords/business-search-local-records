Methodology

Records come from the public building-permit files published by each city. A row is included when the city lists an issue date inside the published week.

Permit type is the city’s own type label. Trade is assigned when the permit type or the scope of work contains a trade term such as roofing, electrical, or plumbing. One permit can match more than one trade.

On a city page, each permit type shows ten records. Those ten follow the ranking you choose: job value, address, or date issued. Job value is the amount the city published. Where the city left the amount blank, the index says “Not published.”
